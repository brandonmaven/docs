# Connecting Opayo

Maven charges through Opayo (Elavon) Direct using your **vendor name**. Opayo authenticates Direct integrations by vendor name plus IP allow-listing.

## Connecting in Maven

In the [Maven Dashboard](https://app.trymaven.com), open your app → **Payments** tab → **Opayo** card:

- **Environment**: Sandbox or Production
- **Opayo Vendor Name**: your Opayo vendor account name
- **Default Currency**: optional — falls back to `GBP` when a session has no currency

You can also click **Copy link** to send a single-use hosted form so your customer enters their own credentials.

## Processor Response Fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `opayo_vps_tx_id` | Opayo VPSTxId |
| `approval_code` | bank authorization code |
| `opayo_security_key` | SecurityKey (needed for later refund/void) |

You must add Maven's outbound IPs to your allow-list in MyOpayo, and your vendor needs a MOTO merchant account. Opayo Direct does not currently support standalone tokenization, so tokenize mode is unavailable.
