# Connecting QuickBooks Payments

Maven charges through the Intuit QuickBooks Payments API. You provide your Intuit app credentials plus an OAuth token pair; Maven refreshes tokens automatically.

## Connecting in Maven

In the [Maven Dashboard](https://app.trymaven.com), open your app → **Payments** tab → **QuickBooks Payments** card:

- **Environment**: Sandbox or Production
- **Client ID**: your Intuit app client ID
- **Client Secret**: your Intuit app client secret
- **Access Token**: initial OAuth access token (from the Intuit OAuth Playground)
- **Refresh Token**: initial OAuth refresh token

You can also click **Copy link** to send a single-use hosted form so your customer enters their own credentials.

## Processor Response Fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `quickbooks_charge_id` | QuickBooks charge ID |
| `quickbooks_status` | `CAPTURED` on success |
| `quickbooks_card_id` | vaulted card ID (tokenize mode) |

Maven refreshes and rotates your OAuth tokens automatically when they expire. Generate the initial token pair from the Intuit OAuth 2.0 Playground for your app.
